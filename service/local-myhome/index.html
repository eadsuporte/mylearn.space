Frequently asked questions

What changes, and what stays the same?

Do I need to change my Moodle theme to use MyHome?

MyHome is a local plugin for Moodle 4.5 or later and works independently of Moodle core. Your theme and plugins should be validated before production deployment.

Can the front page use my organisation's colours, copy and images?

Yes. The plugin lets you customise featured areas, banners and other front-page content to present a consistent brand.

Will learners see their courses and progress?

The plugin considers accessible courses and learner enrolments. Progress depends on Moodle completion tracking; if the data isn't available, the page won't invent a percentage.

Can I organise programmes into learning paths?

Yes. Learning paths can be linked to cohorts. Course visibility respects permissions and active enrolments, and cohort synchronisation may need configuring.

Is this a visual change or a complete implementation?

MyHome focuses on the front page. We can also review the platform configuration, branding, course structure and other needs to define the implementation scope.
