Mobile Learning

A lighter, branded Moodle™ app for students

MoodleMobile is designed for organisations that want a direct mobile experience around their own Moodle™ platform, without sending students through a generic app identity.

The app connects to Moodle™ using Moodle web services, lets students log in with their existing credentials and gives quick access to courses, grades and profile information. The attached app structure also includes mobile views for course formats and supported learning resources, with offline handling for selected content types.

  • Student login using the Moodle™ mobile service token flow.
  • Course list, course pages, grades and profile area.
  • Support for branded login, icons, splash screen and app colours.
  • Offline download flow for supported resources and activities.
  • Push notification structure for opening links or Moodle™ activities.
