LET'S MAKE IT SIMPLE

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still have questions? Let's talk ↗
I already run Moodle. Can you help?+

Yes. We can review your existing platform and help with hosting, support, upgrades, performance, customisation or integrations, depending on your needs.

Do I need to purchase every service?+

No. You can start with one specific need or combine several services. We define the scope after understanding your situation.

Can the platform reflect my brand?+

Yes. Customisation can include the theme, colours, navigation and course presentation. A branded mobile app is also an option.

How does pricing work?+

We discuss your audience, current environment and goals, then prepare a proposal with scope and terms suited to the project.

Can you train our team?+

Yes. Training for teachers, instructors and administrators can be included, covering course configuration and Moodle features.
